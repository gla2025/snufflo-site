Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Snufflo ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use the Snufflo app, why we collect it, and how we use it. We comply with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Who we are

Snufflo is operated as a sole trader under the trading name "Snufflo". If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at nottinghamdigital@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

We collect only the data we need to provide the service:

  • Account information — your name and email address when you sign in with Apple or Google.
  • Location data — your approximate location while using the app, to show you nearby dog-friendly places. We do not store your location history.
  • Content you submit — if you submit a place, we store the name, address, description, photos, and any other details you provide.
  • App usage data — basic analytics to understand how the app is used (e.g. which screens are visited). This data is aggregated and not linked to you personally.

3. How we use your data

  • To provide and operate the Snufflo service.
  • To show you dog-friendly places near your location.
  • To allow you to submit and manage places.
  • To send you important service-related communications (e.g. if a place you submitted is approved or rejected).
  • To improve the app based on aggregated usage patterns.

We rely on legitimate interests (providing the service you requested) and consent (for location access, which you can withdraw at any time in your device settings) as our lawful bases for processing.

4. Third-party services

Snufflo uses the following third-party services, each of which may process your data under their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google LLC) — authentication, database, and file storage. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Maps Platform — map display and location search. Google Privacy Policy
  • Sign in with Apple (Apple Inc.) — optional sign-in method. Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In (Google LLC) — optional sign-in method. Google Privacy Policy

We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising.

5. Data retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data is deleted within 30 days. Place submissions you have made may remain in the app as they form part of the shared community database, but will no longer be linked to you personally.

6. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Withdraw consent (e.g. location access) at any time via your device settings.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at nottinghamdigital@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Children

Snufflo is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data, please email us at nottinghamdigital@gmail.com.
